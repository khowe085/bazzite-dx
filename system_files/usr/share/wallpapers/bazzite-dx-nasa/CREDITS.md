# bazzite-dx NASA wallpapers

The default desktop slideshow of bazzite-dx: pictures from NASA's Hubble and Webb photostreams on Flickr and from NASA Earth Observatory's Image of the Day, chosen in September 2026. Each file is the original its source published, unmodified, except where a note says otherwise. Each folder is a Plasma wallpaper package whose `metadata.json` carries the same title, credit, licence and link as this list.

Left out: pictures with people in them, annotated images, charts, spectra, maps and data plots, artist's concepts, and anything smaller than 2880 px on its long side. Five Webb originals are PNGs over GitHub's 100 MB file limit and too large for Plasma to show (Qt will not decode an image that needs more than 256 MB, about 67 megapixels, unless the format decodes at a smaller size, as JPEG does). They are scaled to cover a 3840×2160 screen; their notes say so.

NASA does not endorse this project.
